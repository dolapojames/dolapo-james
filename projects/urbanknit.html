Urbanknit: Fashion Accessories e-commerce

Urbanknit is a independent fashion accessory business focused on unique, handmade home and fashion accessories primarily sold on online.

As the owner and creator, I launched a Wordpress/Woocommerce website to establish an online prescence. I decided on this CMS and template route in order to focus more on other aspects of the business. This included all the details for branding the business; creating the logo and brand colour palettes, designing packaging, print and promotional material, product photography and more. In addition, writing copy for the website as well as a monthly newsletter.
